## Agent skills

### Encyclopedia work

Before changing content or catalog metadata, read `docs/architecture.md` and
`docs/editorial.md`. For research or factual corrections, read `docs/research.md`.
For business investigations or resuming one, read `docs/workflows/wayfinder.md`.
For contributor responsibilities and handoffs, read `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
Use `GLOSSARY.md` for canonical domain terms. Write educational content in Persian
with precise English equivalents. Preserve installed skills and their lockfile.

### Infrastructure checks

After changing metadata, taxonomy, templates, or navigation, follow
`scripts/README.md`. Structural passes do not establish factual quality or human
publication approval. After implementing a ticket, apply the global
`ticket-walkthrough` skill before handoff; keep human evaluation pending until
observations arrive.

### Issue tracker

Issues and specs live as local Markdown under `.scratch/<feature>/`.
See `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md`.

### Triage labels

Use the five default triage roles as status strings.
See `docs/agents/triage-labels.md`.

### Domain docs

Single-context: root `GLOSSARY.md` and `docs/adr/`.
See `docs/agents/domain.md`.
